Can My Words Predict My Rating?

I trained two models on my reviews: a Ridge Regression to predict the exact star rating, and a 3-class Logistic Regression to classify each review as Meh (≤2.5★), Solid (3.0–3.5★), or Great (≥4.0★). Features include TF-IDF text vectors combined with VADER sentiment scores.
